Privacy policy

This policy applies to Rinvue Inc. and covers how we collect, use, protect, and disclose information in connection with our death verification platform and website. If you have questions, contact privacy@rinvue.com.

1. Who we are

Rinvue Inc. ("Rinvue," "we," "us," "our") is a software-as-a-service company headquartered in Houston, Texas. We operate a death verification platform that connects regulated organizations to Electronic Death Registration System (EDRS) data from all 50 states and the District of Columbia, and to the federal Social Security Death Master File (SSDMF).

2. Information we collect

From client organizations

  • Organization name, address, and industry classification
  • Authorized user names, work email addresses, and job titles
  • API credentials and access log data
  • Billing and contract information

Query data submitted through the platform

  • Names, dates of birth, Social Security numbers, and other identifying information submitted by clients for verification purposes
  • Match results and confidence scores generated by our platform
  • Timestamps and metadata for each verification query

Website visitors

  • IP address, browser type, and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Cookie identifiers (see our Cookie Policy for details)

3. How we use your information

  • To provide, operate, and improve our death verification services
  • To authenticate authorized client users and manage account access
  • To process verification queries against EDRS and SSDMF data sources
  • To generate and maintain immutable audit logs for regulatory compliance
  • To communicate with you about your account, service updates, and support requests
  • To comply with legal obligations including SSA DMF certification requirements, HIPAA, GLBA, and applicable state laws
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized access to our platform

We do not use client query data for any purpose beyond the contracted verification service. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process personal data on the following legal bases: performance of a contract with your organization; compliance with legal obligations (including SSA, HIPAA, GLBA, and state vital records law); legitimate interests in operating a secure and reliable verification platform; and, where applicable, your consent.

5. Data sharing and disclosure

We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • State EDRS systems and SSA SSDMF: Query data is transmitted to registry systems solely for verification purposes under our data sharing agreements
  • Subprocessors: Cloud infrastructure (AWS GovCloud), security monitoring, and other service providers operating under signed subprocessor DPAs. A current list is available to clients upon request
  • Legal compliance: When required by law, court order, or regulatory authority
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, with appropriate confidentiality protections

6. Data security

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational security measures including AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, zero-trust access controls, multi-factor authentication, role-based permissions, immutable audit logging, and annual third-party penetration testing. We are SOC 2 Type II certified and maintain HIPAA-aligned security practices.

7. Data retention

We retain verification query logs and audit records for a minimum of 7 years in compliance with insurance industry regulatory requirements. Client account data is retained for the duration of the contract plus 3 years. Raw death certificate data accessed under the Raw Data tier is subject to enhanced retention and deletion terms specified in the applicable Data Processing Agreement.

You may request deletion of personal data subject to our legal retention obligations by contacting privacy@rinvue.com.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Data portability (where technically feasible)
  • Withdraw consent where processing is consent-based
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact privacy@rinvue.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act. We do not sell personal information. You have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion, and to opt out of any future sale (which we do not conduct). Frequently asked questions

How does the 30-second data refresh work?
Rinvue maintains persistent, monitored connections to EDRS data feeds in all 50 states and the SSDMF. Our pipeline ingests, normalizes, and indexes new records every 30 seconds. If a registry sync is delayed, stale-data alerts fire within 60 seconds and our on-call team is automatically paged. Your API responses and dashboard always reflect the most recent sync cycle.
What is the difference between match results and raw data?
Match results return a verified confirmed/no-record/pending response with a confidence score. No raw PII from the registry is transmitted. Raw data access — available at additional cost under the Raw Data tier — provides the full underlying death certificate fields including date of death, state of filing, and where available, cause of death. Raw data requires a signed DPA, BAA (for healthcare clients), and demonstrated independent SSA DMF compliance.
Which industries does Rinvue serve?
Rinvue serves any regulated organization with a legitimate need for death verification data: life and P&C insurers, banks and lenders, pension fund administrators, healthcare organizations, government agencies at the federal and state level, and legal or estate services providers. Our compliance stack is designed to satisfy the regulatory requirements of all these industries.
How do I set up SSO for my organization?
We support Microsoft Azure AD / Entra ID, Okta, Google Workspace, OneLogin, and Ping Identity via SAML 2.0 and OIDC. Email integrations@rinvue.com with your identity provider details and we will provide metadata and configuration instructions. SSO setup typically takes 1–2 business days.
What compliance documents do you provide during vendor due diligence?
We provide our SOC 2 Type II report (under NDA), penetration test executive summary, completed VSA/SIG questionnaires, DPA template, BAA template (healthcare clients), subprocessor list, cyber insurance certificate, and security policy overview. Contact legal@rinvue.com to request the full due diligence package.
Why does a record show "No record" in EDRS or SSDMF?
Registry records can take 2–10 business days to propagate after a death certificate is filed at the state level, and SSDMF updates from SSA can take additional time. If a record remains unverified after 10 business days, contact support@rinvue.com for a manual registry check.
How do I request API sandbox access?
Email integrations@rinvue.com with your organization name and intended use case. Sandbox credentials with synthetic EDRS and SSDMF test records are provisioned within one business day. No DPA is required for sandbox access.
